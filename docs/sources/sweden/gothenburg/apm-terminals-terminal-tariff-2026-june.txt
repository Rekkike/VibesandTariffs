|  2 | Yard Storage (per TEU per day) |  | SEK  |

Yard Storage is calculated per calendar day.

Storage calculation Export &amp; Transhipment units

Export &amp; Transhipment units have the same Port Storage calculation. Calculation of Port Storage time starts on the day export container is gated into the terminal or transhipment discharged. Calculation ends the day before container is loaded on a vessel.

Storage calculation Import units

Calculation of Import Port Storage time starts the day after container discharge and ends when container departs from the terminal via gate or rail.

Storage Calculation Domestic units

Calculation of Port Storage time starts the day container is gated into the terminal and ends the day container is departing.

Temperature controlled units, units containing dangerous goods, and OOG units have the same free Port Storage terms as described above.

Important: Additional yard surcharges apply to this type of units as per Section 3, Yard Surcharges.

Detailed storage calculations can be found on https://www.apmterminals.com/en/gothenburg/services/terminal-tariff.

# Payment Conditions

Conditions of Payment Payment terms are 20 days from issue date of invoice, in accordance with Terms of Business.
Interest on overdue payments will be charged in accordance with the Swedish Interest Act.
Please quote invoice number when making payment.

# Determination of Weights and Measurements

Charges are calculated on the basis of weights and measurements received in accompanying documentation or an average in general use in commerce. For any cargoes weighed and/or measured by the terminal, charges are calculated on the basis of the figures obtained. APM Terminals Gothenburg withholds the right to charge surcharges according to this tariff in case the figures obtained is obviously wrongly given.

11

# Invoice dispute process

All disputes against invoices should be made within 8 days of receipt of the invoice to receivables.got@apmterminals.com. Please check the website for the updated instructions.
An invoice re-handling request is not considered a dispute.
