1  General provisions
1.1 Provisions for use of facilities
Along with current statutory regulations, use of facilities shall be governed by the regulations 
contained in these Prices and Conditions and by the Standard Terms and Conditions of Business 
of the EUROGATE Group in its latest version.
1.2 Orders for use of facilities
Orders must be given for use of facilities; such orders must be made to the Company
concerned in electronic form.
1.3 Charges
1.3.1 Gross weights and dimensions shall be used for calculation. Packaged weights over 10 t must be 
declared separately.
1.3.2 The weight shall be rounded up to a full 100 kg.
1.3.3 Dimensions shall be rounded up to a full 0.1 cbm.
1.3.4 Minimum charge: not applicable
1.3.5 The prices specified in this Statement are for Container and packages in seaworthy packaging of 
a kind customary in the industry and are applicable only if the goods are not subject to special 
provisions of the GGV-See (ordinance on transport of hazardous substances by seagoing vessels) 
or other legal provisions.
1.3.6 For services which are not indicated in this Statement or which require special expenditure, 
charges must be agreed upon with the Company in advance.
 (e.g. salvage of containers)
1.3.7 Charges apply on weekdays Monday to Friday for the First and Second Shift. Work outside these 
hours is possible on request.
 Extra charges will be levied for work outside these times.
1.3.8 All charges are net amounts in terms of the VAT Act. In case that tax-free sales for shipping are 
claimed this can only be effected by an official certificate of the tax authorities. This certificate 
has to be presented in advance.
1.3.9 As a rule, the charges and expenditures shall be collected in arrears; however, the Company 
shall be entitled to require advance payments.
For goods in storage passing into other hands, charges up to the date when transfer of 
ownership takes place shall be paid immediately.
1.3.10 Invoices shall be payable immediately upon receipt. Incoming payments shall be set off against 
the oldest accounts receivable. After 7 days, arrears interest may be charged, amounting to 5 % 
above the discount rate of the European Central Bank
1.3.11 Creation and dispatch of additional copies of invoices is subject to a fee of € 60.00 per copy of 
the invoice.
1.3.12 All services not mentioned in the list of prices and conditions are charged at cost.
1.3.13 A 1.5% social fund will be charged on all services.                                                             
The following shall be excluded:
-storage charges
-materials for stowing and lashing
-security charge

1.4 Liability for payment
1.4.1 The liable debtor shall be the client, unless particular exceptions are stated below.
1.4.2 For the use of the quay by a seagoing vessel, charges shall be made to the ship’s agent.
1.4.3 Handling charges for goods via quay shall be made to the following:
a. For outgoing sea traffic, to the issuer of the port record, or in other cases to the earlier client;
b. For incoming sea traffic, to the recipient of the cargo, or in other cases to the earlier client;
c. For ship’s equipment and provisions to the ship’s agent.
1.4.4 If the shipping company assumes payment of cargo handling charges, the client shall remain 
liable for payment along with the shipping company.
1.4.5 Storage charges shall be made to the respective client. In the case of import cargo this shall be 
for the whole storage period, even should the cargo be released later.
1.4.6 If, in the case of goods in transit (land/land container), the right of disposal changes, the first 
client shall remain liable for payment of charges.
1.4.7 Charges for any services not specifically mentioned shall be made to the respective client.
1.4.8 The acceptance of the order implies that the client has agreed on direct debit in advance.
1.4.9 Any offset against the Company’s accounts receivable is hereby excluded, unless the
counterclaim put forward for offset is uncontested or has become res judicata
1.4.10 The Processing of invoices will be done by electronic mail having attached a PDF-File. In addition 
the parties may mutually agree to transmit invoice data by UN EDIFACT message INVOIC.

2 Vessel charges
Vessel charges will be calculated for the use of handling facilities by a seagoing vessel in
accordance with:
- The gross tonnage (GRT) and lay time of the vessel (berthing charge)
- The volume of cargo loaded / discharged (quay dues).
Charges shall be paid by the shipping company or its agent. The provisions of chapter 1,
Paragraph 1.4 shall not be affected thereby.
2.1 Berthing charge / tonnage dues
Every ship berthed at the handling facilities must pay a berthing charge. Berthing charges shall 
be based on the gross tonnage index* of the vessel concerned and shall amount to the 
following, multiplied by the gross tonnage index:
2.1.1 for the first 24 hours of lay days minimum 1,04 €
2.1.2 for every additional 12 hours or part of this period 0,60 €
The lay time commences at the time of docking and shall be calculated uninterruptedly until the 
time of casting off.
2.1.3 Vessels without proper Tonnage Measurement (barges, lighter etc.) are to be charged per 
commenced 24 hours: On request
per running meter of the vessel
2.1.4 Ships using the Container Terminal as a lay by berth prior start of cargo
operation or after completion of cargo operation by whatever reason, are
subject to a charge of:
Per 24 hrs or 
part thereof 
/TEU           
1,34 €
Berth dues counts by maximum nominal intake of TEU (twenty foot equivalent unit)
*) Law of 22 January 1975 on International Convention on Tonnage Measurement of Ships of April 23, 1969
2.2 Quay dues / weight dues
Quay dues shall be applied to goods loaded / discharged via the quay:
a. Vessels engaged in overseas shipping (outgoing and incoming
traffic) 9,45 €/t
b. Vessels engaged in European shipping (incoming and
outgoing traffic) and internal waterway traffic 5,46 €/t
Quay dues a) – b) are also charged on goods handled
• from the quay into watercraft, or from watercraft to the quay
• from the quay for a seagoing vessel, using a floating crane; or from a seagoing vessel to the 
quay, using a floating crane
c. For the use of a container crane for re-stowing on board:
charges as set out in Section 4, plus quay dues of 7,46 €/t
