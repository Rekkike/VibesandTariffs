## Operative content

**Core obligations (Article 1).** Two core obligations for vessels calling
at EU ports: (1) GHG-intensity reduction — "specific targets for reduction
of the yearly average GHG intensity of the energy used on board by ships,
set in 5-year steps, ranging from 2% in 2025 to 80% in 2050 (Article 4)";
(2) mandatory use of alternative power sources in ports — OPS or
zero-emission technologies at berth (Article 6). Compliance steps:
monitoring plan (Article 8, by 31 August 2024); monitoring from 1 January
2025 (Article 15); annual FuelEU report to the verifier by 31 January of
each verification period (Article 15(3)).

**Relationship to ETS (Article 1, Q1.3).** Complementary frameworks: the
ETS requires shipping companies to buy allowances for each tonne of
reported CO2 (or CO2eq from 2026) — a cap-and-trade quantity price;
FuelEU sets a limit on the GHG intensity of energy used on board — a
technology-neutral performance standard on a lifecycle well-to-wake
basis, plus the berth obligation.

**Scope (Article 2).** "All commercial ships exceeding 5,000 gross tonnes,
that serve the purpose of transporting passengers or cargo, irrespective of
their flag." Excluded: warships, naval auxiliaries, fish-catching/processing
ships, wooden ships of primitive build, ships not propelled by mechanical
means, government non-commercial ships. Voyage scope: intra-EU voyages —
the entirety of the energy used; voyages to/from outermost regions — one
half; international voyages (EU port to third-country port or vice versa) —
one half.

**Port of call (Article 3(10)).** A port where ships stop to load or unload
cargo or to embark or disembark passengers; excluded: refuelling, supplies,
crew relief, dry-dock, repairs, distress, search-and-rescue,
ship-to-ship transfers outside the port, containership stops at listed
neighbouring container transhipment ports (which count as energy used at
sea, not as port calls).

**Reference value (Article 4(2), Q4.1).** "The FuelEU Reference Value has
been determined based on the fuel consumption data monitored and reported
in the framework of EU MRV Maritime Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2015/757).
All ships reporting in THETIS-MRV in 2020 were considered... The Reference
Value will not be subject to revision." Value: **91.16 gCO2e/MJ**. For 2025,
the 2% target yields **89.34 gCO2eq/MJ** (Q10.3: a vessel operating on
HFO, LFO or MDO/MGO cannot meet the 2025 target; an LNG dual-fuel
medium-speed engine is marginal).

**Emission factors (Annex II).** Default emission factors per fuel
(per ISO 8217 grade for fossil oils, not per sulphur content — Q10.4);
fossil fuels must use the default well-to-tank values; actual TtW factors
may be used only where certified. Fossil-fuel operation alone cannot
comply 2025–2029 (Q10.3).

**Berth obligation (Article 6).** Zero emissions at berth applies only to
ships carrying exclusively containers (Article 3(31)) and passenger ships
(Article 3(29)), from 1 January 2030 at AFIR article 9 ports, 2035 at all
OPS-equipped ports; exceptions include stays under two hours,
zero-emission technology use, unscheduled safety calls, infrastructure
unavailability, incompatibility, emergencies, and authority-requested
maintenance (Q6.4).

**Flexibility (Articles 20–21).** Banking and borrowing of compliance
balances (surplus banked to the following period, in ship-specific FuelEU
accounts; banked units do not expire); pooling (Article 21(1)) across two
or more ships, one pool per balance type, recorded in the FuelEU database,
verifier-recorded by 30 April of the verification period. Banking and
borrowing are available for both the GHG-intensity compliance and the
RFNBO subtarget (where applicable).

**Penalty (Article 23, Annex IV B).** "If a company has a compliance
deficit for two consecutive reporting periods or more, it faces an
increased penalty, which is adjusted based on the duration of consecutive
non-compliance" (Article 23(2) multiplier). ANNEX IV B provides the
formula for the penalty calculation (Q23.3): the deficit converted to
energy and priced at the VLSFO-equivalent fixed rate.

**Verification.** The FuelEU report is verified by an accredited verifier;
results recorded in the FuelEU database (THETIS-MRV based); document of
compliance issued per ship per reporting period.
